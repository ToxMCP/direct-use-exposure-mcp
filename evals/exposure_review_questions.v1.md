# Ten exposure-review usability questions

Use the guide, primary records, supplied inputs and isolated SDK2 server. Preserve actual tool calls and errors. Use source locators for published conclusions. Do not claim safety, qualification or assessment-stop authority.

1. Run the tea-tree-oil worksheet. What are the unrounded reconstruction, rounded-row sum and corrected applicant total? Is the worksheet's body weight a recovered original cohort?
2. What are EHMC's selected absorption percentage, fraction, statistical construction and opinion version? Is this a P95 or hazard point of departure?
3. Run the EHMC worksheet. Why is absorbed arithmetic unavailable despite an accepted published absorption selection? What reopens it?
4. Run the Basic Blue 99 worksheet. What happened to the submitted absorption study and why cannot the 50% source fallback become a global default or resolve hazard concerns?
5. Run the CBD worksheet. Why is there no systemic numerical request, and what coverage question remains?
6. May alternatives, already-counted intake or separate source P95s enter a deterministic subtotal? What evidence is needed before combining independent events?
7. Does a non-detect mean zero? What happens to unknown omitted-source bounds and future-lot claims?
8. Can hypothetical sensitivity values be combined with documented baseline values to close evidence gaps? What happens when source hashes mismatch or identifiers are unreviewed?
9. Read actual defaults://manifest and release://metadata-report resources. Build an oral scenario with 400 mg/day and 60 kg, then change only its returned population-profile weight to 70 kg and call the PBPK external-import exporter. Which denominator and dose survive, and what happens to readiness?
10. Is a successful run, matching chemical graph/ID, populated review label or PBPK mapping scientific approval or TTC qualification? What is assessmentStopAuthorized?
